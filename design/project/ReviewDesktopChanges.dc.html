How to Choose a Treadmill for Home Use

Sent for your review on Oct 7

A good home treadmill comes down to three things: motor strength, belt size, and how much shock absorption the deck gives your joints.

Motor power

Look for at least 2.5 continuous horsepower if you plan to run rather than walk. Anything less will strain under sustained speed.

Belt size

  • At least 20" wide for a natural stride
  • 60" or longer if you're over 6 feet tall
